Privacy Policy

This policy covers data handled when you visit the Galaxy Gym website or contact the club through a link on it.

Last updated · November 23, 2026

On this page

  • Browsing the website
  • Data on your device
  • Contacting the club
  • External services
  • Your rights and complaints

Browsing the website

The website provides general information about the club. You can browse it without a club account. Membership enquiries and payment arrangements are handled directly by the club.

The pages run without forms that send data to the gym, analytics or trackers. The hosting provider may process your IP address and request data to deliver the website and operate and protect its service.

Data on your device

The workout planner saves selected exercise IDs, sets and repetitions in this browser until you reset the plan or clear the site’s data.

The My account page saves what you enter yourself in this browser: your name if you add it, your memberships with their dates and the amount if you enter it, each workout’s date, name, duration and note, weight readings, and the details you save from the fitness calculator (age, height, weight, activity, goal and body fat or muscle). There is no account on a server. This data is not sent to the gym or anywhere else, and it stays until you remove it on the page or clear the site’s data. You can download a copy of it, or delete all of it, in the My data section.

Fitness calculations run in your browser, including the age and measurements you enter. Workout plan data and calculator inputs stay in your browser. The website does not save calculator inputs to its local storage, except when you press “Save my details to my account”.

The website keeps a small marker in your browser’s session storage so the opening screen plays only once per visit. The marker is cleared when you close the tab.

The admin panel used by the gym’s staff keeps its edits and photos in the browser of the device it is opened on, and sends nothing to a server.

So the website works without a connection, your browser keeps a copy of the pages and files you open (and of every page once you install the app on your home screen). This copy holds none of the data you enter, updates itself when you are online, and is removed when you clear the site’s data.

Contacting the club

You can call 079 108 1877 or message us on WhatsApp or Instagram. The club receives the contact information and enquiry details you share so it can respond. Contact the club for details about how it retains correspondence.

External services

If you choose a WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or Google Maps link, that service opens and its own privacy policy applies. The WhatsApp enquiry link opens a draft with any membership or visit preferences you choose, for you to review before sending. The website serves its images and fonts from the same host as its pages.

Your rights and complaints

Under Jordan’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023, you can request access, correction or erasure of your data, object to processing or withdraw consent where applicable. Contact the club on 079 108 1877 or through Instagram to make a request.

If the club does not address your complaint appropriately, you can escalate it to the Personal Data Protection Directorate at the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship. The available channels are on the Ministry’s services page.
